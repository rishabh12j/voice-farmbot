GrowMate.

A garden you can talk to.

For people who love gardening but find bending, lifting and remembering hard.

Try saying…

“Water the tomatoes.
“Take a photo of the lettuce.
“Go home.
“When should I plant basil?
“What's the weather like today?
“Stop.

Use your own words — it understands plain English.

STOP

Always one press from stopping.

  • A big red button on the side stops everything at once.
  • You can also just say the word “stop.”
  • The robot can never move outside its bed.
1

You say what you want.

“Water the basil.” In your own words.

2

It tells you what it heard.

“Water the basil — yes?” Correct it before anything moves.

3

It does the gardening.

The robot rolls to the right plant and waters, photographs or checks it.
